1. Title: Back-End Integrable On-Chip MIM Decoupling Capacitors Featuring High Capacitance With Ultra-Low Leakage Current by Nitrogen-Incorporated HfZrOx
Authors: Kuen-Yi Chen, Teng-Chuan Chen, Ruei-Wen Kao, Yan-Xiao Lin, Kuan-Ying Hsieh, Yung‐Hsien Wu
Year: 2019
Core Content: This paper reports the use of HfZrOx (HZO) deposited by plasma-enhanced ALD and annealed at 400 °C as the dielectric in on-chip MIM decoupling capacitors. The study finds NH3 plasma treatment at the HZO/TiN interface and on bulk HZO effectively suppresses interfacial scavenging and passivates oxygen vacancy-related defects, leading to a high dielectric constant (k=28.9), high capacitance (23.3 fF/μm2), and ultra-low leakage current (0.16 fA/μm2 at 1 V, 25 °C). The treatment also improves the reliability and lifetime of capacitors. The processes (HfO2, ZrO2, NH3 plasma treatment) are fab-friendly and scalable for sub-7 nm technology.
Relevance: Directly relevant as it addresses leakage current reduction in HZO-based MIM capacitors through specific interface and bulk defect passivation techniques.

2. Title: (Invited) Fabrication Technique of Ferroelectric HfxZr1−XO2 Thin Films Using ALD-ZrO2 Nucleation Layers
Authors: Takashi Onaya
Year: 2023
Core Content: The paper studies ferroelectric Hf0.43Zr0.57O2 thin films fabricated with atomic layer deposited ZrO2 nucleation layers. Insertion of ZrO2 nucleation layers (ZrO2-NLs) significantly improved the remanent polarization and dielectric constant of TiN/HZO/TiN capacitors. The ZrO2 layers also contributed to lower leakage current densities and superior endurance compared to films without these layers. Improvements are attributed to epitaxial-like grain growth and larger total dielectric thickness.
Relevance: Highly relevant as it focuses on improving electrical properties, including reducing leakage current, in HZO-based MIM capacitors by using ZrO2 nucleation layers.

3. Title: Nonvolatile Applications and Reliability Investigation of La-Doped ZrO2 Antiferroelectric Capacitors
Authors: Jianguo Li, Junliang Zhou, Wenchao Yan, Zibo Dong, Yuetong Huo, Choonghyun Lee, Zeping Weng, Yi Zhao
Year: 2025
Core Content: This study demonstrates the nonvolatile behavior of La-doped ZrO2 antiferroelectric capacitors with enhanced reliability. Key findings include a unique polarization reversal mechanism leading to a unidirectional imprint effect, ultralow coercive voltage, high endurance over 10^12 cycles, and stable retention characteristics at elevated temperature. A built-in electric field was engineered via work function modulation.
Relevance: Related to ZrO2-based antiferroelectric capacitors and their reliability, which could indirectly influence leakage current reduction strategies through material doping and field engineering methods.

These three papers collectively contribute insights and practical methods for reducing leakage current in HZO and ZrO2 based MIM capacitors, focusing on plasma treatment, nucleation layer engineering, and material doping/reliability enhancement respectively.
